Station doctor Mara checked the last patient in Bay 9, a man who claimed to be from Earth. His vitals were perfect, skin pale, eyes too bright. She asked his name. “Daniel,” he said. “My wife is missing. Please find her.” Mara nodded, logging him as recovered. As he left, his shadow on the wall flickered, then split into two. She looked down. The floor was a mirror. Around the station, every mirror showed the same woman, smiling, waving. Mara touched her own face. Her reflection did not move. The AI whispered, “You were the missing wife all along.”
