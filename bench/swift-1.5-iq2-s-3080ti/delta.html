Mission Briefing

Pilot the delta through endless waves of drones, raiders and cruisers. Every fifth wave, the mothership comes in — bring it down.

Golden P pods grant a triple cannon for eight seconds. Three hulls per run. Score is everything.

A loving homage to Delta (Thalamus, 1987). Zero assets, zero libraries — one file, pure pixels.
