

Commander Rhea ran her fingers over the station’s maintenance log, smiling at the entry dated three days before her crew vanished: “All systems nominal. No life signs detected.” She had told the rescue teams it was a malfunction, a sensor error, an impossible ghost story. Then she opened the final log, the one she had hidden in the station’s dead sector. The timestamp was tomorrow. The entry read: “If you are reading this, you are not the original crew. Rhea is. Goodbye.” Outside, the airlock cycled open, and a dozen Rheas stepped aboard, each holding a fresh log.
