Technical notes

Simulation runs on a fixed 120 Hz timestep with sub-stepped collision, so the ball can never tunnel through a block no matter how fast sector nine gets. Paddle contact maps the hit offset to a ±65° launch cone, which means the edge of the paddle is always the risky, interesting shot. Every sound in this cabinet is synthesised live — there are no audio files anywhere on the board.
