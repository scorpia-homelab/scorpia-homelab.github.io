THE ONE RULE OF DELTA

01Your arrowhead is weak alone. Weapon pods drift in from the right on a steady course — line your hull up with one and it snaps into formation behind you, adding its gun, its colour, and one extra hit of armour.

02The bay holds three. Want a better pod? Hit X to jettison the rear module first. Pods are also your airbag: take a shot with a full bay and you lose a pod instead of a life.

03Fuel burns constantly — engines and trigger both. A white TANKER doesn't take a bay slot, it just tops you up. Run dry and your guns go cold in the middle of a Warden's fire pattern. That is how everyone loses.
