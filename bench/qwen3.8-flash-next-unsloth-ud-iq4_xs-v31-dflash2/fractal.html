01

How it is computed

Each pixel seeds the recurrence zn+1 = zn² + c. On Plate I the pixel supplies c and the orbit starts at z₀ = 0; on Plate II the pixel supplies z₀ and c is held fixed.

Iteration stops the moment |z| > 256 — past 2 the orbit provably escapes to infinity, and the extra head-room keeps the colour ramp continuous. The fractional part of n + 1 − log₂log|z| supplies the smooth band, so no contour stripes appear. Edges where the colour index jumps are re-sampled 2×2.

02

Why the two rhyme

Plate I is an index of Plate II. A parameter c belongs to the Mandelbrot set exactly when the critical orbit of z² + c stays bounded — which happens exactly when the Julia set Kc is connected.

So: hold the cursor inside the black body and Plate II is one piece. Step outside onto the filaments and it shatters into a Cantor dust of dust-sized islands. The badge in the hinge reports which, live.
