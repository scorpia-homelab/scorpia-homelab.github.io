    Operating manual

    Read the wall before you break it

    1. Never let it land twice

      You start with three balls. A lost ball keeps the wall exactly as broken — the wall never heals, so every chip counts.

    2. Hit off-centre to steer

      The paddle reflects by contact offset: strike near the cap and the ball leaves at 60°. Centre hits send it straight up into the same groove.

    3. Chain for the multiplier

      Every brick before the ball returns to the paddle extends the chain. Three bricks = ×2, up to a hard ×9. The chain snaps the instant the paddle touches the ball.

    4. Armoured blocks take three hits

      Blue takes two, bone-white takes three, and steel takes nothing — it just sparks. Amber blocks detonate and take their neighbours with them.

    5. Stack cells deliberately

      Pierce plus a chain is the fastest route to ×9. Laser plus wide paddle is the safest way to survive a sector you can't read yet.
