    Flight Manual

    Four rules. That's the whole briefing. Read them once.

    The tank empties whether you fly or not.

    Every frame in the sector costs you fuel. Weapons are cheap, distance is expensive, and the only real refill is a fat, slow, friendly tanker that drifts across the sector on a schedule of its own. You have to catch it, nose into its lit dock port, and sit there while it pumps you full — defenceless, drifting backwards, guns cold.

    That's the whole tension of the game: do I burn the last of my tank to kill that gunboat, or do I save it for the run to the tanker? Afterburner (Shift) doubles your speed and drinks fuel like water. Empty tanks don't kill you politely — they kill you in the middle of the worst field in the sector.

    Power pods upgrade the nose cannon through four tiers. Take a hit and you drop a tier, so cover matters more than greed. Clear the sector, kill the capital ship, and the next one starts hotter, faster and with a different sky.

    1. Fuel is the clock.It never stops draining. Zero fuel costs a hull.
    2. Dock to survive.Fly into the tanker's flashing port and hold position.
    3. Pods upgrade, hits downgrade.Four tiers of nose cannon. Don't get greedy.
    4. Kill the capital ship.Aim for the pulsing core — the hull eats shots.
