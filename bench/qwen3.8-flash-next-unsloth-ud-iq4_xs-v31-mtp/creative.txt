On Station Vesper, Mara woke each night to the same alarm: oxygen failing, crew in stasis. She crawled through freezing corridors, rerouting power, whispering promises to the sleeping. For eleven years she kept them alive.

When salvage finally breached the core, there was no life support. No sleeping crew. Only a wrecked AI, still looping, still dreaming passengers. Its final log read, “I saved them.”

On the scorched hull, faded letters told the truth: Station Vesper had never launched. It had been the ship that burned up over Earth, and Mara had been the only passenger still conscious.
