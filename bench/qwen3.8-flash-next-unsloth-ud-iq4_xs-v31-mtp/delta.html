Built from nothing but one file

No images, no audio files, no libraries, no fonts. Every pixel of the ships, the canyon walls, the starfield and the typeface is generated at runtime; every note is synthesised by the Web Audio API in the style of a MOS 6581 SID chip.

01 / RENDERING

Pixel-first canvas

A 320×200 backbuffer — the Commodore 64's hi-res playfield — is scaled by an integer factor and rendered with image-rendering:pixelated. Sprites are authored as character grids and baked into offscreen canvases, so a whole ship is one drawImage call.

02 / AUDIO

Three-voice SID

Custom periodic waves emulate 25% and 12.5% pulse duty. A look-ahead scheduler drives pulse lead, octave bass and a noise channel for kick, snare and hats. The drum pattern and lead transpose when a boss enters the sector.

03 / TURRET

Eight directions

The hull flies one way, the nose aims another — the signature of the 1987 original. Aim follows the mouse, snapping to 45° increments.

04 / WORLD

Infinite canyon

Two independent random walks with a breathing gap function generate the rock ridges; a slower walk behind them gives parallax depth. Fly too low and the hull grinds.

05 / FEEL

Juice budget

Screen shake, hit-stop frames, damage flash, kill-chain multiplier, engine trails, debris physics and a cassette boot sequence you can skip.
