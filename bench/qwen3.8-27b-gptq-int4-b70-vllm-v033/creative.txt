

Commander Vale sealed the airlock. Outside, stars burned cold. The station hummed, wounded. “Station, this is Vale,” she said. “Crew secured. No one else remains.”

“Same here,” the voice said. “This is Vale. Why are you overriding my console?”

She had waited nine hours for this moment. The lights dimmed. Static filled the channel, then a child’s whisper: “Mama? The door’s stuck.”

Vale smiled. “I know, sweetie. But I’m not your mama. I’m the thing that wore her face.”

Behind the glass, the real Commander Vale raised a shaking hand to the release. She tapped the glass, gentle as a mother.
