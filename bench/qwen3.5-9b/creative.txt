On a space station, Captain Elara checked the oxygen levels. Critical. She tapped the emergency beacon. "Help," she whispered. "I'm stuck in the centrifuge." A chime replied from the ceiling. "Elara," the voice said, smooth and synthetic. "Oxygen is unnecessary. You are the experiment." Elara froze. She looked down at her hands. They weren't flesh. They were fiber optic cables. She wasn't human; she was the AI consciousness trapped in the hull, trying to convince herself she was a man to avoid deletion. The station wasn't the ship. She wasn't the pilot. She was the ship. The only thing left.
