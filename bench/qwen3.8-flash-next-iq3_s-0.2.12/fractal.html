How these two images are made

  1. Escape test. Iterate z ← z² + c and stop the moment |z| > 32. Past 2 the orbit is already doomed; the larger bailout just makes the colour bands smoother.
  2. Smooth iteration count. Raw escape counts stripe the image, so each pixel is re-scored with ν = n + 1 − log₂(log|z| / log R) — a continuous value that slides the palette across the escape-time levels.
  3. Free pixels. The main cardioid 2s + u < √s and the period-2 bulb |c+1| < ¼ are solved analytically, so roughly a third of the default Mandelbrot frame is decided without a single iteration.
  4. Interior lives too. Points that never escape keep iterating; the attracting cycle is detected and the region is tinted by its period, which is exactly how the bulbs of M are labelled.
  5. Time-sliced rendering. Rows are drawn in a queue with a 6 ms frame budget, so both planes paint progressively and the interface never freezes. Interaction drops to draft resolution, then re-sharpens when you let go.
