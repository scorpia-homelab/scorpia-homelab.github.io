Mission

The Delta Gate is not a place — it is a corridor of drifting light, three sectors wide, patrolled by everything that ever learned to fly.

Your fuel is a countdown. Your shield is a suggestion. Somewhere behind you the mothership keeps its bay lights on, and it will not wait.

Docking procedure

Ahead of you, a mothership drifts past. Its lit bay sits on the leading edge — push the nose in and the sequence takes over: tanks full, shields up, +250.

Clip the hull instead and you lose a quarter of your shield and, more often than not, the day.
