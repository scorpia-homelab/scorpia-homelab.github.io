    The Brick Codex

    Every block in the grid is drawn from one of five classes. Learn the hit counts before the third row of steel shows up.

    Standard

    Single-hit stock. Colour shifts per row so you can read the grid depth at a glance.

    1 hit60 pts

    Armored

    Two or three hits. Each hit darkens the face and cracks the shell — the last one shatters it.

    90 pts

    Steel

    Indestructible. Route around it, or clear everything else — the level ends without it.

    ∞ hits5 pts

    Gold

    One hit, big payout, and it always spits out a power capsule. Hunt them early — they sit deep in the stack where the multiplier is hardest to build.

    Guaranteed drop240 pts

    Chain Multiplier

    Every brick you break without the paddle touching the ball adds to the chain. Three links per multiplier step, capped at ×9, and the meter drains after 3.5 seconds of silence. One paddle touch resets it to nothing.

    Resets on paddle contactup to ×9

    Operator Notes

    Four habits that separate a 4,000-point run from a 40,000-point run.

    01

    Never center the ball

    A vertical ball is a dead ball — it can only ever hit one column. Aim the paddle edge to keep the ball crossing the grid diagonally.

    02

    Open a corridor early

    Carve one vertical shaft down to the bottom rows. Once the ball is under the stack, chains run long and the paddle barely has to move.

    03

    Save the laser for steel

    Cannons can't move steel but they clear armored rows fast. Hold Space and walk the paddle beneath the thick blocks.

    04

    Shield is a timer, not a wall

    Each save burns 1.2 seconds of the field. Don't rely on it — treat it as one extra life you can spend twice.
