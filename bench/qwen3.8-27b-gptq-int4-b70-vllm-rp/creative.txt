

Docking Bay 7 hummed. Kira wiped condensation from the viewport, checking the station’s slow spin. Outside, a tiny satellite blinked, then died.

“Signal lost,” she said into the comms. “All systems nominal.”

Static answered. Then a voice she hadn’t heard in years, warm and human: “Kira? It’s me. I made it.”

Her breath caught. The station groaned, lights flickering. On the screen, a face appeared—her mother, smiling, framed by Earth’s blue curve.

Kira laughed, tears freezing on her cheeks.

“Mom, I told you I’d keep the station safe. But I never told you I was the only one left alive.”
